Design Philosophy

Built for actual handheld use.

Every decision in HUDRA starts from one question: does this get in the way of playing? If the answer is yes, it gets cut.

🎯
Overlay, not launcher

HUDRA isn't a dashboard you live inside. It's a floating panel that appears when you need it, adjusts what you need, and vanishes. Double-tap the tray icon or hit your hotkey and you're back in the game in seconds.

🎮
Controller-first navigation

D-pad, stick, shoulder buttons — every control in HUDRA is reachable without touching the screen. Designed for the moment your hands are already on the device and you don't want to reach for anything else.

⚡
Instant hardware response

Scroll a TDP slider and the hardware responds immediately. No Apply button, no confirmation dialog. HUDRA talks directly to your silicon and trusts you to know what you're doing.

🪟
Modern WinUI 3 foundation

Built on WinUI 3 and .NET 8. Fluent design, smooth animations, proper touch targets. HUDRA doesn't look like a debug tool that escaped the lab — it looks like it belongs on your device.

Home · Settings
System Controls & FPS Limiter

Volume, brightness, resolution, refresh rate, HDR toggle, and battery status — all on the Home page. Plus RTSS-powered FPS limiting and configurable power profiles that switch automatically when gaming starts.

HUDRA scans Steam, Epic, GOG, and other launchers automatically. Cover art pulls from SteamGridDB. Per-game profiles remember your preferred TDP, fan curve, AMD settings, and FPS cap — applied automatically when a game launches and restored when it exits.
